About Us

A Home From Home For Your Children

Established in 1993, Little Farm Breakfast, Afterschool and Holiday Club is a family-run childcare provider serving families throughout North Somerset.

With facilities set in a beautiful rural area and a team of fully qualified staff, our setting was graded OUTSTANDING by Ofsted in both 2009 and 2014 for the exceptional quality of childcare we provide.

We offer high-quality childcare that gives parents complete peace of mind, knowing their children are in a safe, nurturing, and stimulating environment.
